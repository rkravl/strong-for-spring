Progression and recovery

Weeks 1–2: use manageable loads, possibly 2 sets, with 3–4 reps in reserve. Weeks 3–4: build to listed sets. Weeks 5–8: gradually increase loads only when form is controlled and symptoms remain absent. Rest about 60–90 seconds between sets. Creatine monohydrate: typically 3–5 g daily. Protein needs depend on overall diet and training.
